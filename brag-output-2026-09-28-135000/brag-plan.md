# FarmConnect launch video plan

## Angle
Show the owner’s journey from livestock and supply management through a request, then show how suppliers respond.

## Hook
“Your farm, one connected workspace.”

## Screens and highlights
- Owner dashboard: livestock, feeds, medicines and requests.
- Livestock list and available medicines.
- Feed browse and request form.
- Owner request tracking and supplier approve/reject actions.

## Punchline
“Connecting farmers with quality suppliers.”

## Tone and visual identity
Warm, polished, practical. Source palette of deep greens, pale green and cream; serif display type and clean sans-serif UI labels.

## Storyboard (20 seconds, landscape)
- 0–2.4s: owner dashboard and key work areas.
- 2.4–4.8s: livestock records and management.
- 4.8–7.2s: medicine browse and request action.
- 7.2–9.6s: available feeds and request action.
- 9.6–12s: request form, livestock selection and quantity.
- 12–14.4s: owner request statuses.
- 14.4–17.2s: supplier request review, approve or reject.
- 17.2–20s: FarmConnect close and product tagline.
